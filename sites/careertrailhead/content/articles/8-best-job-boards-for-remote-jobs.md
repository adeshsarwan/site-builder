Remote job boards fall into three groups: paid screened boards, free remote-only boards, and the big general boards with a remote filter bolted on. Each group fails in a different way, so most people searching seriously end up using two or three at once rather than picking a winner.

This guide compares eight of them on cost, what they are genuinely good at, and the limitation you will hit after a week of use. Pricing and availability change, and some boards restrict listings by country or by US state, so check the current pages before you pay for anything.

> Key takeaways
> 
> - Use FlexJobs when you want pre-screened listings and will actually use the subscription — its annual plan is billed at $5.95/month, paid as $71.40 up front (FlexJobs, 2026-05-12).
> - Use We Work Remotely's free Basic membership to browse, save listings and follow companies at startups and small remote-first teams (We Work Remotely, undated).
> - Use LinkedIn's Remote workplace-type filter with a saved job alert to catch roles from companies that never post on remote-only boards.

## Detailed Breakdown

**FlexJobs.** This is a subscription board built around screening. FlexJobs markets full access to 100% verified remote jobs plus job search tools, with plans starting at $9.95/month (FlexJobs, 2026-05-12). Entry options include a 14-day full access trial for $2.95 that auto-renews at $23.95 every 4 weeks if you do not cancel, a 3-month plan at $9.95/month billed as $29.85 up front, and an annual plan at $5.95/month billed as $71.40 up front (FlexJobs, 2026-05-12). The screening claim is the platform's own description of its process, not an independently audited one — treat it as a reason to expect fewer scam listings, not a guarantee of none. FlexJobs is most useful if your field is administrative, healthcare-adjacent, education, bookkeeping or project management, where remote-only free boards are thin. Set a calendar reminder for the day before any trial renews.

**We Work Remotely.** The site reports 6M+ monthly visitors and a 90% fill rate on job posts, and its counter showed 42,261 jobs posted at time of access (We Work Remotely, undated) — all platform-reported figures, so read them as marketing to employers rather than your odds of hearing back. Employers pay $299 to post a job (We Work Remotely, undated), which filters out casual listers. For job seekers, a free Basic membership covers browsing, saving listings, uploading a resume and following companies, but an account is required to browse and apply at all (We Work Remotely, undated). Paid tiers add volume: Pro at $14.95/month on a 12-month commitment, with a $2.95 first month, unlocks unlimited applications, up to 5 custom job alerts and subscriber-only events, while TopAccess at $29.95/month on a 12-month commitment with no free trial adds partner marketplace access to Toptal, Arc and PeoplePerHour, unlimited Toptal resume tailoring and eligibility for a performance reward of up to $500 (We Work Remotely, undated).

**Remote.co.** A smaller, cleanly organised board that lists openings in categories such as remote accounting, customer service, entry-level and freelance jobs (Remote.co, 2024-09-26). Its value is browsing by function when you do not yet know the right job title to search for — useful if you are switching fields and the keyword you would type is the wrong one. Volume is the trade-off; check it weekly rather than daily.

**LinkedIn.** The reason to use it is not the board but the companies on it. Filter job search results by workplace type and select Remote, then save the search as a job alert so new postings arrive by email. Add a second alert with a tighter title so you are not reading the same generic feed twice. Two cautions: employers choose the workplace-type tag themselves, so hybrid and in-office roles leak into remote results, and claims that applying within the first hours improves your odds are anecdotal or drawn from platform-published posts, not settled evidence. Apply when your application is tailored, not when a timer says so.

**Indeed.** Best for raw coverage, especially in customer support, claims, scheduling, billing and sales roles that rarely appear on remote-only boards. Type Remote in the location field or use the Remote filter, then save the search and turn on email alerts from your account. Expect duplicates, agency reposts and listings that stayed up after the role closed. Scan the posting date before you spend time on a long application form.

**Remote OK.** A fast, dense feed organised by tags, with an RSS option if you prefer to read listings in a reader rather than a browser. Good for engineers, designers and growth marketers who want to see what posted today in under five minutes. Pay ranges shown on listings are estimates generated by the site, so do not quote them back to an employer as if they came from the company.

**Wellfound.** Startup-focused, and the useful part is that company profiles often publish compensation and equity ranges on the listing, which saves you a screening call to find out the band. Applications frequently reach a founder or a small hiring team directly. The limitation is structural: early-stage companies pause hiring, and job seeker profiles are the primary application route, so a thin profile is a real handicap here.

**Working Nomads.** A curation layer rather than a source. You pick categories and receive email digests, which makes it the lowest-effort way to keep a passive search alive while you are employed. Because it aggregates, many links lead back to boards you already check.

One constraint that cuts across all eight: a listing marked remote may still be restricted to specific countries, or to particular US states where the employer is registered to run payroll. That rule varies by employer and by state, and it is an employment and tax question rather than something a job board can resolve — read the posting's location line before applying and ask the recruiter directly if it is ambiguous.

## Where to Access Each Tool

- **FlexJobs** — the official pricing and plans page for current plan costs and renewal terms, then your account's subscription settings page to set or cancel renewals.
- **We Work Remotely** — the remote jobs category index page to browse, plus the membership comparison page for Basic, Pro and TopAccess tier details.
- **Remote.co** — the remote jobs board category index pages (accounting, customer service, entry-level, freelance and the rest) rather than the homepage.
- **LinkedIn** — the Jobs tab search results page, with the on-site/hybrid/remote workplace filter set to Remote; then the saved jobs and job alerts management page to manage alert cadence.
- **Indeed** — the job search results page with Remote in the location field or the Remote filter applied; then the My Jobs and saved search pages in your account to enable email alerts.
- **Remote OK** — the per-tag listing pages for your role or stack, plus the RSS feed URL if you read listings in a feed reader.
- **Wellfound** — the job seeker signup and profile editor first, since applications run through the profile, then the jobs search page.
- **Working Nomads** — the email digest signup page, choosing categories individually rather than subscribing to everything.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a paid remote job board worth it if free boards exist?+**

It depends on whether your field is well served by free boards. If you work in engineering, design or growth marketing, the free remote-only boards carry most of what a paid board would show you. If you work in administration, bookkeeping, education or healthcare support, a subscription like FlexJobs — $9.95/month on the 3-month plan or $5.95/month on the annual plan, billed as $71.40 up front (FlexJobs, 2026-05-12) — buys you screened listings in categories the free boards barely cover. Decide after a month of free searching, not before.

**Should I pay for an upgraded tier on a free board?+**

Only if you have hit the specific cap the upgrade removes. On We Work Remotely, Pro at $14.95/month on a 12-month commitment lifts the application limit and adds up to 5 custom job alerts, while TopAccess at $29.95/month on a 12-month commitment adds partner marketplace access and resume tailoring (We Work Remotely, undated). A 12-month commitment is a long bet on a search you hope will be short, so check the cancellation terms on the membership page first.

**How many boards should I actually use at once?+**

Two or three, chosen for different coverage: one remote-only board for roles at distributed companies, one general board with a remote filter for employers who post nowhere else, and optionally one digest for passive monitoring. More than that and you spend your search time reading duplicates. Give each one a fixed slot in your week rather than checking all of them daily.

**Do boards that advertise a high fill rate mean I'm more likely to get hired?+**

No. We Work Remotely's reported 90% fill rate and 6M+ monthly visitors describe employer outcomes and site traffic (We Work Remotely, undated), and they are figures the platform publishes about itself. A high fill rate tells you listings are real and roles get closed; it says nothing about your individual chances against the other applicants in the same pool.

**What should I tailor before applying through any of these boards?+**

Match the job title in the posting to the title line on your resume where it is honest to do so, mirror the three or four skill terms the posting repeats, and put a number on your two most relevant bullet points — volume handled, cycle time, budget, headcount. On boards like Wellfound where a profile is the application, make those edits in the profile itself, since that is what the hiring team reads.
