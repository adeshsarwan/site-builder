Real estate sales is one of the few occupations you can enter with a high school diploma and a state licence, but the median annual wage for real estate sales agents was $52,830 in May 2025 (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2025), and 54% of agents are self-employed, so that income is commission-based rather than salaried. Salaried corporate work generally asks for a bachelor's degree first: the median for business and financial occupations was $82,660 in May 2025, against $50,980 for all occupations (BLS, 2025).

This guide compares the two on pay, entry requirements, hours, outlook and ceiling, then lists the signals that point to each. Figures are United States federal data; pay, licensing and employment rules differ by country and by state, so check your local licensing body and current wage data before you commit.

> Key takeaways
> 
> - Pay: agents earned a median of $52,830 and brokers $73,220 in May 2025 (BLS, 2025); corporate management analysts earned a median of $101,860 and computer and information systems managers $175,140 (BLS, 2025) — but those corporate roles require a bachelor's degree, and the manager role also 5+ years of experience.
> - Entry cost: a real estate licence needs 40-180 hours of prelicensing education and $75-$485 in fees depending on the state (Homes.com News, undated); the corporate path usually starts with a multi-year degree.
> - Fit: choose agent work if you can absorb irregular commission income and want to set your own schedule including evenings and weekends; choose corporate if you want payroll pay and an occupation projected to grow 10-16% over 2025-35 rather than 2% (BLS, 2025).

## Setting the Context

**The real estate agent path.** A sales agent is licensed to sell property but must work under a broker, and usually earns a portion of the commission on each property sold (BLS, 2025). Typical entry-level education is a high school diploma or equivalent (BLS, 2025). Every U.S. state requires a licence, and the usual conditions are being at least 18, holding a high school diploma or equivalent, completing prelicensing courses and passing an exam (BLS, 2025). The coursework load varies sharply: 40 hours in Alaska and Vermont up to 180 hours in Texas, with total fees from $75 in South Carolina and Wisconsin to $485 in Colorado (Homes.com News, undated). Licences then renew every 2 to 4 years, and most states require continuing education to renew (BLS, 2025).

Your starting title is sales agent or salesperson, affiliated with a brokerage. Within five years the standard step up is a broker's licence, which most states gate behind at least 2 years of experience as a licensed sales agent (BLS, 2025). Brokers can run their own business or work as an associate broker managing an independent office inside a larger company (BLS, 2025). That self-employment is the norm, not the exception: 55% of brokers and 54% of sales agents are self-employed (BLS, 2025). Median pay reflects the step: $52,830 for sales agents and $73,220 for brokers in May 2025 (BLS, 2025), with the two groups combined at $57,400 (BLS, 2025).

**The corporate path.** "Corporate job" covers salaried, payroll roles inside a company — analyst, coordinator, specialist, associate — usually entered with a bachelor's degree and sometimes an internship. Two BLS occupations show where it leads. Management analysts, which requires a bachelor's degree, had a 2025 median pay of $101,860 per year and 1,077,100 jobs in 2025 (BLS, 2025). Computer and information systems managers, which requires a bachelor's degree plus 5+ years of related work experience, had a 2025 median of $175,140 and 685,800 jobs (BLS, 2025). The group median for business and financial occupations was $82,660 in May 2025, above the $50,980 median for all occupations (BLS, 2025).

The practical five-year shape differs. In real estate, progression is a licence you qualify for after 2+ years and then a business you own. In corporate work, progression is internal promotion to a role like the manager position above, where the stated requirement is a degree plus five or more years of related experience (BLS, 2025). Titles, hiring norms and credential rules vary by employer and country.

## Signs Each Path Is Right for You

**Signs the real estate agent path fits**

- You can cover living costs through a stretch with no closings, because pay is commission-based and 54% of sales agents are self-employed rather than salaried (BLS, 2025).
- You want to start earning without a degree: the typical entry education is a high school diploma or equivalent plus 40-180 hours of prelicensing coursework costing $75-$485 in fees, depending on the state (BLS, 2025; Homes.com News, undated).
- Evening and weekend availability is an advantage to you, not a sacrifice — those hours are standard in the role, and many agents set their own schedules (BLS, 2025).
- You would rather run a business than be promoted inside one: after at least 2 years as a licensed agent in most states you can qualify for a broker's licence and open your own firm (BLS, 2025).
- You are comfortable chasing third parties — escrow companies, lenders, home inspectors, pest control operators — to keep a deal on track before closing (O*NET OnLine, 2025).
- You can treat a 2-to-4-year licence renewal cycle with continuing education as routine admin rather than a burden (BLS, 2025).

**Signs the corporate path fits**

- A fixed monthly figure matters more to you than upside: the business and financial occupations median was $82,660 against $50,980 for all occupations in May 2025 (BLS, 2025).
- You already hold, or are willing to complete, a bachelor's degree — the stated entry requirement for management analysts (BLS, 2025).
- You want to work in an occupation that is expanding rather than flat: 10% for management analysts and 16% for computer and information systems managers over 2025-35, versus 2% for brokers and agents (BLS, 2025).
- You are willing to spend 5+ years building related experience in one field, the benchmark attached to a $175,140 median management role (BLS, 2025).
- You prefer scheduled, employer-set hours to a calendar driven by client availability on evenings and weekends (BLS, 2025).
- Your household budget, visa status or mortgage application depends on documented payroll income rather than variable commission.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which pays more after five years?+**

On medians, the corporate path. A real estate sales agent who earns a broker's licence — possible after at least 2 years of licensed experience in most states — moves from a $52,830 median to a $73,220 median (BLS, May 2025). A corporate management analyst role, entered with a bachelor's degree, had a 2025 median of $101,860, and a computer and information systems manager role requiring 5+ years of experience had a median of $175,140 (BLS, 2025). Medians hide the spread, though: agent pay is commission-based, so individual earnings depend on market, brokerage split and deal volume. Check current BLS wage data and local brokerage commission splits for your area.

**Which is easier to break into without a degree?+**

Real estate, clearly. Typical entry-level education for brokers and sales agents is a high school diploma or equivalent (BLS, 2025), and the licence requires being at least 18, completing prelicensing courses and passing an exam (BLS, 2025). That coursework is 40 hours in Alaska and Vermont up to 180 hours in Texas, with fees from $75 in South Carolina and Wisconsin to $485 in Colorado (Homes.com News, undated). Management analyst and computer and information systems manager roles both list a bachelor's degree as the entry requirement (BLS, 2025).

**Is the real estate job market still growing?+**

Barely, on federal projections. Employment of real estate brokers and sales agents is projected to grow 2% from 2025 to 2035 — slower than average — an increase of 8,300 on a base of 530,600 jobs (BLS, 2025). O*NET lists 420,900 sales agent jobs in 2024 with 3-4% projected growth to 2034 (O*NET OnLine, U.S. Department of Labor, 2025). The access point is turnover, not growth: about 40,400 openings a year are projected, mostly from people transferring occupations or leaving the labor force (BLS, 2025).

**Can I test real estate while keeping my corporate job?+**

Partly. Most brokers and agents work full time, but some work part time and many set their own schedules, with evenings and weekends common (BLS, 2025) — which is when a salaried employee is free. You still need the licence first: 40-180 hours of prelicensing study and the state exam, at $75-$485 in fees (Homes.com News, undated). Before you start, check your employer's outside-work policy and your state licensing body's rules on holding a licence while employed elsewhere, as both vary.
